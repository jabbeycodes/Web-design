# Site Vision: showme.sites

ShowMe Sites is a high-end web development agency landing page. The goal is to convert visitors (founders, businesses) into clients for landing pages, marketing sites, and full-stack apps.

**Brand:**
- Name: showme.sites
- Parent: ShowMe (shwme.app)
- Tone: Professional, confident, results-oriented
- Colors: Dark background (#0a0a0a), amber/gold accent (#f59e0b), white text

**Target Audience:**
- Founders building MVPs
- Small businesses needing websites
- Creators needing landing pages
- African businesses needing local + global presence
